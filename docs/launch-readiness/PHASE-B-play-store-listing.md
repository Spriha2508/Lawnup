```
Grow something beautiful. 🌿

LawnUp is an AI-powered plant care companion built for India — your home garden, balcony pots, and terrace plants, all looked after in one place. Scan a plant to identify it, diagnose what's wrong, and get clear, season-aware care advice tuned to your city's weather and air quality.

— IDENTIFY ANY PLANT —
Point your camera at a plant and LawnUp recognises it in seconds, with its common Indian name, scientific name, and a confidence score. Not sure if it's healthy? LawnUp also checks for visible disease and stress.

— MEET DOC. SAGE, YOUR PLANT EXPERT —
Ask anything — yellowing leaves, watering, pests, soil, repotting, propagation — and get grounded, practical answers. Doc. Sage draws on a curated knowledge base of common Indian plants, so the advice fits Indian homes, seasons, and conditions.

— CARE THAT KNOWS YOUR CITY —
LawnUp factors in live weather and air quality (AQI) for your city, plus the Indian seasons — summer, monsoon, and winter — so watering and care tips actually match what your plants need right now.

— SOIL & LIGHT ADVICE —
Get the right soil mix for your plant and a quick read on whether a spot has enough light, before a plant struggles.

— YOUR GARDEN, REMEMBERED —
Save your plants, give them nicknames, and get gentle watering reminders so nothing gets forgotten. Track your growing collection over time.

— BUILT FOR INDIA —
Hundreds of common Indian household, garden, kitchen, and balcony plants — money plant, tulsi, snake plant, hibiscus, curry leaf, rose, areca palm, and many more — with names and care written for Indian conditions.

WHAT YOU GET FREE

• Weather & AQI-aware care
• Watering reminders

PREMIUM (optional)

Whether you're a first-time plant parent or a seasoned gardener, LawnUp helps you understand your plants and keep them thriving.

Download LawnUp and grow something beautiful. 🌱
```
