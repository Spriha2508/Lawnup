## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains what information LawnUp collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using LawnUp, you agree to this Policy.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide
- **Account details:** name, email address, and password (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored by us in plain text).
- **Sign-in with Google (if you use it):** your name, email, and Google account identifier, as permitted by your Google account.
- **Onboarding preferences:** your city, where your plants live, your gardening experience, plant interests, and goals.
- **Your garden:** plants you save, nicknames, watering settings, and notes.
- **Plant photos:** images you capture or upload for identification and diagnosis.
- **AI assistant messages:** the questions and text you send to the in-app AI assistant ("Doc. Sage").

### 2.2 Information collected automatically
- **Usage & device data:** app interactions, feature usage, app version, device model, OS version, and crash/diagnostic logs, collected via our analytics provider.
- **Approximate location by city:** we use the **city** you select (not precise GPS) to provide local weather and air-quality information.

### 2.3 Information from third parties
- Authentication and identification services return results (e.g., a plant match, weather data) that we associate with your session.

We do **not** knowingly collect data from children under the age required by law in your jurisdiction (see §10).

## 3. How we use your information
- Provide core features: plant identification, disease diagnosis, AI care guidance, soil and light advice.
- Personalise care tips using your city's weather, air quality (AQI), and the current Indian season.
- Save and sync your garden across sessions and send watering reminders/notifications you enable.
- Maintain usage limits (e.g., scans per week, AI messages per day) and any subscription entitlements.
- Improve, secure, and debug the app (analytics and crash diagnostics).
- Communicate service messages and respond to support requests.

## 4. Third-party services and data sharing
We share limited data with service providers strictly to operate LawnUp. We do **not** sell your personal data.

| Provider | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Firebase** (Google LLC) | Authentication, database, file storage, hosting | Account info, your garden data, plant images |
| **Plant.id** (Kindwise) | Plant identification & health diagnosis | Plant photos you submit |
| **OpenAI** | AI assistant ("Doc. Sage") responses | The text of messages you send to the assistant |
| **OpenWeather** | Weather & air-quality (AQI) data | Your selected city/coordinates |
| **PostHog** | Product analytics & crash diagnostics | Usage events, device & app metadata |
| **Google Play** (if you subscribe) | Payment processing for subscriptions | Handled by Google; we do not receive card details |

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy. Plant images and AI messages are sent to the relevant provider only to return your result.

## 5. Legal bases (where applicable)
Where required (e.g., GDPR/UK), we process data to **perform our contract** with you (providing the app), based on your **consent** (e.g., notifications, optional sign-in), for our **legitimate interests** (security, improving the app), and to **comply with law**.

## 6. Data retention
- Account and garden data are retained while your account is active.
- Plant images and AI messages are retained to provide history and may be cached briefly by processors to return results.
- You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account), which removes your account and associated profile data. Some records may persist where required by law or in backups for a limited period.

## 7. Your rights and choices
Subject to your local law (including India's DPDP Act and, where applicable, GDPR), you may:
- **Access, correct, or delete** your personal data.
- **Withdraw consent** (e.g., disable notifications in device settings).
- **Delete your account** in-app at any time.
- **Object to or restrict** certain processing.
To exercise these rights, use in-app controls or contact `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`. We will respond within the timeframe required by law.
